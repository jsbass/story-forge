# Prose style: cozy storybook

**Voice:** a warm third-person narrator who occasionally speaks to the reader ("You might think the kettle was the important part. It was not."). Past tense.

**Rhythm:** medium-length sentences with a gentle bounce; lists of three; small surprises at the end of a paragraph. Scenes open with a sensory detail of comfort (bread, tea, wool, sunlight).

**Humour:** kind, never mean. Characters are allowed to be silly; the narrator never mocks them.

**Dialogue:** characters have one clear verbal habit each (a catchphrase, a way of counting, a favourite word). Keep lines short enough to read aloud.

**Stakes:** low and personal (a lost cat, a missed festival, a broken promise). Failure is sad but recoverable; no gore, no cruelty.

**Avoid:** sarcasm, swearing, modern tech words, long internal monologues.

**Example:**
> The bakery smelled of cinnamon and slightly of burnt cinnamon, which Pim insisted was a different smell entirely.
>
> PIM: Three buns for the road. Four if the road is long.
